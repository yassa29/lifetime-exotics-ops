# Facility Readiness Checklist

## Exterior / lot
- [ ] Permanent dealership sign installed
- [ ] Sign visible from road
- [ ] Main business-name lettering meets state requirements
- [ ] Lot can display at least 15 vehicles
- [ ] 3 customer parking spaces clearly available
- [ ] Grass/mud areas not being used as required display surface
- [ ] Trash, scrap, and construction debris removed
- [ ] Entrances/exits safe and usable
- [ ] Exterior lighting working where needed

## Office
- [ ] At least 288 sq ft permanent business facility
- [ ] Desk and chairs
- [ ] Secure storage for business records
- [ ] Business phone available / posted
- [ ] Business hours and days posted
- [ ] Office looks operational, not temporary

## Restroom
- [ ] Working toilet
- [ ] Running water
- [ ] Sink works
- [ ] Restroom clean and accessible

## Utilities / building
- [ ] Electrical service active
- [ ] Electrical inspection / service release complete if required
- [ ] Water active
- [ ] Plumbing leaks addressed
- [ ] Broken glass / openings secured
- [ ] Unsafe exposed wiring removed or repaired
- [ ] Required renovation permits/finals complete

## Dealer-license photo set
- [ ] Street-facing sign
- [ ] Entire building exterior
- [ ] Vehicle display area
- [ ] Customer parking
- [ ] Office interior
- [ ] Restroom
- [ ] Posted hours / phone
- [ ] Main entrance
- [ ] Additional entrances/exits if applicable

## Final inspection walk-through
Do a full walk-through from the perspective of a state inspector. Anything that makes the location look unfinished, temporary, unsafe, or not operational should be handled before the inspection date.
