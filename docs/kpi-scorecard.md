# KPI Scorecard

Track monthly and weekly once open.

## Inventory
- Units in stock
- Average cost basis
- Average days in stock
- Units aged 30+ days
- Units aged 60+ days
- Units aged 90+ days
- Recon dollars per unit

## Sales
- Units sold
- Average front-end gross
- Average gross margin %
- Average days-to-sale
- Cash vs financed mix
- Lead-to-appointment rate
- Appointment-to-sale rate

## Cash flow
- Cash tied in inventory
- Floorplan balance
- Average carrying cost per unit
- Unfunded contracts
- Titles outstanding
- Vendor payables due

## Compliance
- Open title issues
- Open temp-tag issues
- Missing deal-jacket documents
- Insurance / bond renewal dates
- Tax return due dates
- License renewal dates

## Weekly management questions
1. Which 5 vehicles are tying up the most cash?
2. Which units should be repriced today?
3. Which deals are not funded yet?
4. Which titles are still outstanding?
5. Which vendor or recon bill is hurting gross?
6. What is the single biggest thing blocking more sales this week?
