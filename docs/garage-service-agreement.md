# Garage Service Agreement

## Goal

Lifetime Exotics needs a nearby operating mechanical repair facility willing to sign the Tennessee Motor Vehicle Commission Garage Service Agreement because the dealership does not currently operate its own onsite mechanical repair facility.

A body-only collision shop is not the preferred choice. Use a legitimate mechanical repair garage that can perform normal vehicle repair and maintenance work.

## Call script

Hi, this is Yassa with Lifetime Exotics. We’re opening a used-car dealership nearby and Tennessee requires us to have a service agreement with a repair facility. We would send vehicles to you whenever we need mechanical work. Would you be willing to sign the state Garage Service Agreement with us?

## Ask the shop for

- Legal business name
- Physical address
- Phone number
- Owner/authorized representative name
- Signature on the state form
- Approximate distance from dealership

## Notes

The agreement does not mean the repair shop performs work for free or guarantees Lifetime Exotics' vehicles. Repairs are still ordinary paid repair transactions as needed.
