# Lifetime Exotics Launch Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Legal / tax foundation ✅ mostly complete
- LLC formed
- EIN obtained
- Lease / location established
- Zoning letter obtained
- Class 2 business tax license obtained
- Sales & Use Tax registration obtained

## Phase 2 — Dealer-license package 🟡 active
- $50,000 surety bond
- Garage liability / Any Auto insurance
- CPA compiled financial statement
- Ownership Worksheet
- Garage Service Agreement
- Correct sales-tax certificate typo if required

## Phase 3 — Facility 🟡 active
- Electricity restored
- Water / restroom operational
- Office ready
- Permanent sign installed
- 15-car display area ready
- 3 customer spaces ready
- Required photos completed
- Local permit finals / occupancy items completed where applicable

## Phase 4 — State submission 🔴 next
- Salesperson licensing
- Upload complete dealer application package in CORE
- Pay dealer application fee
- Respond quickly to deficiencies
- State facility inspection
- Dealer license issued

## Phase 5 — Inventory engine 🟡 prep now
- Auction memberships
- Floorplan approval(s)
- Repair / recon vendor network
- Transport / tow vendors
- Inventory acquisition rules
- Stock numbering system
- Pricing / aged inventory process

## Phase 6 — Sales operations 🔴 build before first deal
- Buyers Guide process
- Deal-jacket checklist
- Temp-tag process
- Title / registration process
- Cash / financing paperwork workflow
- Funding follow-up
- Customer handoff checklist

## Phase 7 — Scale
- Weekly aged-inventory review
- Gross-per-unit reporting
- Recon cost control
- Lead source tracking
- Lender performance tracking
- Vendor scorecards
- Monthly compliance audit

## Definition of “ready to open”

Lifetime Exotics should not call itself operational until the dealer license is active, the facility is inspection-ready, required insurance/bond are active, at least one salesperson is properly licensed, the deal paperwork workflow is ready, and the team knows exactly how to handle title/tax/temp-tag requirements for the first sale.
