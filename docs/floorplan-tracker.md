## Questions to ask every lender

- Will you approve a brand-new dealership with no operating history?
- Do we need the dealer license in hand before underwriting starts?
- Minimum dealer-license age requirement?
- Personal guaranty required from all owners or only selected guarantors?
- Minimum personal credit score / liquidity requirement?
- Starting credit line?
- Advance rate or max advance per unit?
- Floor fee per vehicle?
- Interest rate / daily interest?
- Audit fees, title fees, wire fees, late fees, extension fees?
- Curtailment schedule?
- Required principal reductions at 30 / 60 / 90 / 120 days?
- Maximum age / mileage / book value per vehicle?
- Can we buy from Copart, IAA, ACV, Manheim, ADESA, independent auctions, and private parties?
- Are rebuilt / branded-title vehicles eligible?
- Do you finance transportation and auction fees or vehicle cost only?
- How fast are purchases funded?
- How are titles held and released after payoff?
- How long does approval usually take?

## Decision rule

Do not choose a lender based only on the biggest line. Compare **true carrying cost**, title-release speed, eligible inventory, curtailments, audit burden, and how friendly they are to a brand-new store.
