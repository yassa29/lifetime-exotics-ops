## Questions to ask every lender

- Will you approve a brand-new dealership with no operating history?
- Minimum dealer-license age requirement?
- Personal guaranty required?
- Minimum credit score?
- Starting credit line?
- Advance rate?
- Curtailment schedule?
- Floor fees / audit fees / wire fees?
- Can we buy from Copart, IAA, ACV, Manheim, ADESA, or private-party sources?
- Are rebuilt / branded-title vehicles eligible?
- How long does approval usually take?
